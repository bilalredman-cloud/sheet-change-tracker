Privacy Policy – Sheet Change Tracker

Publisher: Alahmar Engineering Consultancy, Amman, Jordan · Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Sheet Change Tracker is an add-in for Autodesk Revit that compares the sheets of the open model with a previous revision of the same model and writes an HTML change report with marked-up sheet PDFs. This policy explains what data the add-in handles. The short version: the add-in runs entirely on your computer, collects no personal data, and sends nothing over the internet.

1. Data the add-in collects, how, and what it is used for

The add-in does not collect personal data and contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or account features. It handles only the following data, all of it on your own computer:

DataHow it is obtainedWhat it is used forWhere it stays
Contents of the two Revit models (sheets, views, annotations, grids, structural elements) Read through the Revit API from the model open in Revit and from the previous-revision file you select Comparing the two revisions. Neither model is modified or saved. In memory while the comparison runs
The change report (HTML file and marked-up PDF files) Generated by the add-in from the comparison Showing you the differences between the revisions Written to the folder you choose; you own and control these files
Settings: the last used file paths, selected checks and output options Saved when you run a comparison Pre-filling the window the next time you open it %AppData%\SheetRevisionCompare\settings.json on your computer

The add-in does not read your Autodesk account, licence information, Windows user profile, e-mail address, location or any file other than the two models you select. It makes no network connections of any kind; the only web addresses it contains are links (such as this page) that open in your browser when you click them.

2. Sharing with third parties

No data is transmitted to Alahmar Engineering Consultancy or to any third party. The add-in uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks or cloud services, and Alahmar Engineering Consultancy has no parent, subsidiary or affiliate that receives data. Should any third-party service ever be added in a future version, this policy will be updated first, and any such party will be required to protect the data to at least the standard described here.

Purchases and downloads through the Autodesk Design and Make Marketplace are handled by Autodesk under Autodesk's privacy statement. Alahmar Engineering Consultancy receives from Autodesk only the information Autodesk provides to publishers for order fulfilment and support.

3. Data retention and deletion

Because no data leaves your computer, Alahmar Engineering Consultancy retains nothing. The files the add-in creates (reports, PDFs and the settings file) remain on your computer until you delete them. Uninstalling the add-in removes the program files; you may delete the reports you generated and the settings folder %AppData%\SheetRevisionCompare at any time.

4. Your choices: revoking consent and requesting deletion

You can stop all processing at any time by closing the add-in window or uninstalling the add-in (Windows Settings → Apps → Sheet Change Tracker → Uninstall). As the add-in holds no data about you, there is nothing for the publisher to delete on request; if you contact us for support and share information with us by e-mail, you may ask us at any time to delete that correspondence and we will do so.

5. Children

The add-in is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the add-in changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date and version above will change before that version is published.
